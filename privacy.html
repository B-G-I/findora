  • Impact.com — Affiliate network used by multiple hosting brands we review. Impact.com Privacy Policy →

4. Cookies

Our site uses the following categories of cookies:

  • Strictly necessary: Required for the site to function (e.g., form submission).

Most browsers allow you to refuse, delete, or be notified about cookies. Refer to your browser's help documentation for instructions.
