Effective Date: October 15, 2026  |  Last Updated: October 15, 2026

The short version: Some links on findaura.org are affiliate links. If you click them and make a purchase, we earn a small commission — at no extra cost to you. Our reviews and scores are based entirely on independent testing, not on commission rates.

1. What Is an Affiliate Link?

An affiliate link is a specially tracked URL that lets a vendor know a visitor came from our website. If that visitor makes a qualifying purchase, the vendor pays us a commission — typically a percentage of the sale price or a fixed fee. You pay exactly the same price whether you click our affiliate link or visit the vendor's site directly.

3. How We Test Hosting & Cloud Services

Find Aura's founder — Abderrahmane Anis Nacef — is a full-stack software developer specialising in Laravel, Flutter, Cloud VPS, and scalable web infrastructure. Every hosting product we review is actively tested using the following methodology:

  • Real workloads: We deploy actual Laravel applications or WordPress stores — not just static HTML pages — on each plan we benchmark.
  • Multi-location testing: Speed tests run from at least two geographic nodes (typically EU and US) using tools including GTmetrix, Pingdom, and custom Siege load tests.
  • Extended test window: We test over a minimum of 30 days to capture real uptime and performance consistency — not cherry-picked snapshots.
  • Support interaction: We contact each provider's support team with realistic technical and billing questions and log response times and quality.
  • Independent scoring: Scores and verdicts are assigned based on test results, not on commission rates or commercial relationships.

4. How Affiliate Compensation Affects Our Content

It doesn't — and here's how we ensure that.

  • We do not accept payment for positive reviews. Vendors cannot purchase or influence editorial placement.
  • We review products we would genuinely recommend to our own clients and development community.
  • If a product underperforms in testing, it will receive a low score regardless of affiliate arrangement.
  • We clearly disclose when a link is affiliated (either inline or via the disclosure banners on each article).
  • We maintain editorial independence from all affiliate programmes we participate in.

Affiliate links on findaura.org are typically:
